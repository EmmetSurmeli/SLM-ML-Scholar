WHAT GETS AUTOMATED

Transparent—not a hidden judge

  • Runs the cited extractive answer for every question.
  • Checks abstention, sufficiency, citations, claim support, query coverage, and comparison completeness.
  • Proposes a label, required facts, retained evidence, and corrected answer.
  • Flags explanatory answers that still need synthesis.

Drafts are not human reviews and are never automatically approved as training examples.
